Before You StartHow to Read This Book

Every question here lives in its own file: the question as it was actually asked, the answer worked through in depth — pipeline, trade-offs, a comparison table where one earns its place — and a couple of follow-ups an interviewer would realistically ask next. No question depends on any other; open the contents list below and jump straight to the one you need.

This book has no fixed length. New questions get appended to book.js as a new file lands next to it — the contents list and every page's prev/next nav update themselves from that one list.
